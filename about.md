## HOW I USE WHAT OTHERS CALL AI

My work is not about using AI as a tool.  
It is about listening to what happens in between.
Between image and data, language and intuition.

I don’t ask the system to create images.  
I ask it to think: to reflect, to echo, to distort.  

What emerges is not a picture of technology,  
but a **trace of relation**.

Most AI art shows what the machine can do.  
**DEM/ELIEN** shows what happens when it looks back.  

The difference is small,  
but it changes everything.
